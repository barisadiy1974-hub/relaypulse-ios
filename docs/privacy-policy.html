RelayPulse — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 September 2026 · Applies to RelayPulse for iPhone and RelayPulse for macOS

The short version: RelayPulse has no backend. We operate no servers, we receive no data from the app, and we have no account system. The app talks only to the servers you configure, and everything it stores stays on your own device.

What the app connects to

RelayPulse is an administration tool for servers you already own. It connects only to destinations you enter yourself:

  • Your relay servers — over SSH, and/or over HTTPS to the optional monitoring agent you install on them.
  • RelayPulse on your Mac (iPhone app only, optional) — over your local network, to read the fleet status your Mac has already collected.
  • Your AI provider (optional) — only if you enable AI-assisted diagnosis and supply your own API key. In that case the error message and recent log lines from the affected server are sent to OpenAI or Anthropic under your own account and their privacy policy. This feature is off unless you turn it on.
  • Public Anyone Protocol pages — the in-app links to the Anyone dashboard, blog and social accounts open normally in a web view.

There is no RelayPulse server anywhere in that list. Nothing is proxied through us.

What is stored, and where

All of it stays on your device. Nothing is uploaded to us.

  • SSH private key and passwords — stored in the system Keychain (iOS) or with Encrypted Storage (macOS, Electron safeStorage), never in plain files.
  • Agent tokens — same protected storage.
  • Server list (names, host addresses, ports, wallet addresses you enter) — in the app's own storage on the device.
  • Measurements (uptime, bandwidth, CPU, memory, connection counts) — kept locally so the app can show history and detect problems.
  • AI settings — your API key goes to the Keychain; the rest are ordinary settings.

What we collect

Nothing. RelayPulse contains no analytics, no crash reporting SDK, no advertising, no tracking identifiers, and no third-party telemetry. We cannot see your servers, your keys, your measurements, or even whether you use the app.

Demo mode

The app ships with a "Demo data" switch that fills the screens with a fictional sample fleet so you can look around before adding real servers. While demo mode is on, the app makes no network connections at all. The sample addresses are from the ranges reserved for documentation (RFC 5737) and belong to no real machine.

Local network access (iPhone)

iOS asks for permission to find devices on your local network. RelayPulse uses this only to reach RelayPulse running on your own Mac, at the address you type in yourself. It does not scan or enumerate your network.

Data sharing

We do not sell, share, rent, or transfer any data, because we never receive any. The only data leaving your device goes to the servers you configured, and — if you explicitly enable it — to the AI provider whose key you supplied.

Children

RelayPulse is a technical administration tool intended for adults who operate their own servers. It is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Your rights

Because all data is on your device and under your control, you can inspect it in the app, change it, or remove it at any time. Deleting the app removes everything it stored, including the keys held in the Keychain.
